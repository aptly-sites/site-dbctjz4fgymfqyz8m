A CLEAR NEXT STEP

A few helpful answers.

Start here, and reach out when you need a person.

How do I find an available home?

Browse our available homes, filter by price and home features, and open a listing for photos and details. Contact leasing to confirm move-in timing.

How do I arrange a tour?

Ask leasing about the property you’re interested in. Tour options and instructions will depend on that home.

How do I apply?

Choose a home from our available listings and select Apply to continue to Turman’s application portal. Review the requirements there before submitting.

What is the Zero Deposit option?

The brief describes a lease-insurance option for qualified applicants, with a monthly fee instead of a traditional upfront deposit. Ask leasing about eligibility, costs, coverage, and the terms that apply to your home.

Are pets allowed?

Turman plans to allow approved pets at some properties. Ask leasing to confirm the policy and any applicable conditions for a specific home before applying.

Where can I find my resident account?

Use the resident portal to access your account. Contact accounting@turmanco.com for account-related questions.

Who should I contact about maintenance?

Contact maintenance@turmanco.com or call (706) 689-4545. Ask the office for the current after-hours maintenance process.
