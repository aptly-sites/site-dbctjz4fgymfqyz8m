EXPLORE COLUMBUS

Uptown

Uptown is Columbus’s downtown district, centered on Broadway and the Chattahoochee riverfront. It combines dining, arts venues and outdoor destinations in a compact part of the city.

MidTown

MidTown is a broad Columbus district made up of smaller neighborhoods, historic streets, parks and shopping areas. It is a useful starting point if you want to compare several different residential settings within the city.

Lake Bottom

Lake Bottom, also written Lakebottom, commonly refers to the area around Weracoba/Lakebottom Park in MidTown Columbus. Nearby Weracoba–St. Elmo has a historic residential character, with several early twentieth-century architectural styles.

East Highlands sits in MidTown’s northwest corner, south of Talbotton Road and the Fall Line Trace. Its streets include houses from different building periods, rather than one single architectural era.

A LITTLE PLANNING. A SMOOTHER START.

Your Columbus Move, Step by Step.

  1. Start With Your Everyday Route

    Visit the exact address, test your commute at the times you usually travel, and locate grocery stores and other routine stops. If you use transit, check METRA’s trip planner for your route.

  2. Compare the Full Move-In Picture

    Confirm rent, deposit options, any additional fees, pet policies and move-in dates with Turman. Ask about the Zero Deposit alternative and whether it applies to your chosen home.

  3. Line Up Utilities & Enrollment

    Confirm which services you must arrange at the property. Use Columbus Water Works for water-service requests, check electricity and internet availability at the address, and review school enrollment requirements before moving.

  4. Make Your First Week Easier

    Coordinate keys and arrival with leasing, update your mailing address, document the home’s condition at move-in, and save the resident portal and maintenance contact details.
