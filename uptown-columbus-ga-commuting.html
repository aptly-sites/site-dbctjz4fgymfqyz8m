LIVING IN UPTOWN COLUMBUS, GEORGIA

Getting Around Is the Easy Part.

Restaurants, offices, arts venues and riverfront trails sit close together in Uptown. When the trip takes you farther, compare driving, METRA and airport options from the address of the home you’re considering.

On foot & by bike

Many daily Uptown stops can be reached on foot from the Broadway area. Broadway's restaurants and shops, the Government Center, TSYS, the RiverCenter for the Performing Arts and Golden Park are in or near the Uptown district. Check the walk from your exact rental address.

Walk

The Chattahoochee RiverWalk

This paved trail runs about 22 miles along the river, from Lake Oliver through Uptown toward the Victory District. You can pick it up right in Uptown. Use it for a morning run, an evening walk, or a car-free ride to the National Infantry Museum area.

Walk

Across the river to Phenix City

The 14th Street Pedestrian Bridge links the Columbus RiverWalk to the Phenix City RiverWalk on the Alabama side. You can cross state lines on foot in a few minutes.

Bike

Fall Line Trace

This paved rail-trail connects Uptown to parts of midtown and north Columbus over roughly 10 miles. It offers a paved route for cycling out of Uptown into midtown and north Columbus.

By car

Public transportation: METRA

METRA, the city's bus system, runs 10 fixed routes across Columbus. Route 12 (Veterans Parkway / Civic Center / Victory Drive) serves the Uptown area, and other routes reach Columbus State, Peachtree Mall, Columbus Park Crossing, the Airport Thruway corridor and Fort Benning.

Service hours

Monday–Saturday

About 4:30 AM to 8:30 PM. Hours vary by route. Service varies by route; check the current timetable before traveling.

Accessibility

Dial-A-Ride

Curb-to-curb paratransit is available for eligible riders with disabilities who can't use fixed-route buses. It costs $2.50 per trip. Call (706) 225-4596.

Park & Ride

Park-and-ride lots

The Linwood park-and-ride lot at 900 Linwood Boulevard offers public parking, EV charging, a bike repair station and bike shelter.

Paid & on-demand options

When you'd rather not drive, Uptown residents have several paid options for local trips and for travel out of town.

On demand

Rideshare & taxis

Uber, Lyft and local taxi companies serve Uptown and the airport. Compare prices and pickup details before booking.

Flying local

Columbus Airport (CSG)

About 15 minutes from Uptown. Delta serves CSG; check its current destinations and schedules before booking. Car rental options are available at the airport; confirm hours and availability.

To ATL

Groome Transportation shuttle

Scheduled shuttles to Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport, with with schedules and fares listed by the operator. Book online or call (706) 324-3939. The shuttle departs from 2800 Harley Court in Columbus.

Intercity

Greyhound

Check Greyhound for current stop locations, schedules and intercity routes.
