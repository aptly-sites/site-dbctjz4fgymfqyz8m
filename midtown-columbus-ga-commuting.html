On Foot & by Bike

MidTown includes historic residential streets and several everyday destinations. Walkability changes from block to block, so check crossings, sidewalks and trail access from the rental itself.

Walk

Wynnton Road & 13th Street

These corridors link shops, restaurants and services. Take a scouting walk from a prospective home and compare the crossings you would use regularly.

Park & culture

Weracoba Park & the Museum

Weracoba/Lakebottom Park offers paths and recreation, while the Columbus Museum is another MidTown destination. Check the actual route from your block.

Bike

Fall Line Trace

The paved trail connects parts of Columbus toward the riverfront. Find the nearest safe access point before relying on it for a regular ride.

MidTown includes several smaller historic neighborhoods; the feel of a walk or ride depends on the street and route, not just the neighborhood name.

By Car

Wynnton Road and 13th Street point toward Uptown; Macon Road and I-185 connect other parts of Columbus. Drive times depend on the specific home, traffic and destination.
